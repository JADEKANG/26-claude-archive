## 클립 0

**영상 제목:** How to Use AI Without Getting Dumber
**헤드라인:** (미정)

**Description:**
Using AI made them faster. But 17% dumber.

Anthropic ran a study. Two groups. Same coding assignment.
One group used AI tools. One didn't.
The AI group finished faster — no surprise.
But in a follow-up test, with no AI allowed?
The group that never used AI scored 17% higher.
They struggled through the work. And that struggle became understanding.

The ones who used AI and still performed well?
They weren't using it for answers. They were using it to ask better questions.

It's not whether you use AI. It's how.
Don't let it think for you. Use it to think deeper.

## 클립 1

**영상 제목:** Late to AI, Better at AI
**헤드라인:** The Best AI User Never Used AI Before

**Description:**
The people winning with AI?
They never had to unlearn anything.

Anthropic's Drew Bent spent time in Rwanda and India —
where people are AI native.
First technology they ever used, outside of a phone, was AI.
No baggage. No old mental models. No "this is just a chatbot" bias.

The people who started using AI in 2022?
They're still treating it like an assistant.
Underestimating it. Every day.

The AI native sees what the technology can do today.
And where it's headed tomorrow.
That's the edge.

We all need to think like someone who just picked this up for the first time.
Reset your assumptions. Upgrade your model of the model.

## 클립 2

**영상 제목:** Waste Time Now, Win Later
**헤드라인:** You're Not Pushing Your AI to the Limit

**Description:**
Sometimes AI takes longer than doing it by hand.
That's not a bug. That's R&D.

The most powerful AI users aren't using it for everything.
They're pushing it to its limits.
Doing things that aren't even possible yet.
Building systems that supercharge their workflows.

Spend a fraction of your time just experimenting.
You'll lose a little time today.
But you'll understand what these tools can actually do.

And when the next model drops?
You'll already know what to try.
While everyone else is just catching up.

Today you're wasting time.
Tomorrow you're saving it.

## 클립 3

**영상 제목:** The Skill After Prompting
**헤드라인:** Social Skills Are the Ultimate Advantage

**Description:**
Your New Coworker Is an AI

You spent 15 years learning how to work with people.
Now there's a new type of being in your workplace.
It's showing up in Slack. In your classroom. In your team.

The prompt engineering era? Over.
The new skill isn't technical.
It's social.

The best AI users don't command it.
They collaborate with it.
They treat it like a colleague — understanding how it thinks,
what it needs, how to have a real dialogue with it.

That's how you get more out of AI than everyone else.
Not better prompts. Better relationships.

Practice. Reps. Time spent.
That's how you build social skills with humans.
Same goes for AI.
